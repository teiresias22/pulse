About Pulse

Earth’s last 7 days, live

Pulse replays the last 7 days of earthquakes, tropical storms, US tornado warnings, wildfires, auroras, rocket launches and the International Space Station (ISS) orbit on an interactive 3D globe. Drag the time scrubber or press play to rewind the week. Earthquakes are sized and colored by magnitude, and launches are marked by outcome: success, failure or upcoming. Tropical storms move along their observed tracks, and wildfires are shown while they burn. Tornado warnings appear while they are in effect, and the aurora band follows the Kp index. The ISS position is computed in your browser from its TLE with satellite.js, and the data refreshes live.

Data sources
